How it plays

  • Every hit speeds the ball up. Bounces never slow it down.
  • The ball takes the hitter's colour. If it touches anyone else, they lose a stock. Three stocks each, last one standing wins.
  • Swing to send it back. Your shot goes where you face, and snaps onto an opponent near your aim. Swing late for a perfect hit: extra speed and less time for them to react.
  • The freeze grows with speed. The ball hangs on the bat before it fires. The dotted line shows the shot, including any curve. The hitter can still sweep it up to 45° either way, so move.
  • Hold swing to charge. Keep holding after you connect and the ball stays on your bat, firing up to 40% faster. Everyone gets that time to read your aim.
  • Bunt to reset the tempo. It pops the ball up harmlessly and keeps its speed for the next smash.
